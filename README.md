## DG60FX-112 Preset Library

A small single page application to act as a preset librarian for the Yamaha DG60FX-112 guitar amplifier.
It can also be used for the DG-Stomp.

The Yamaha DG60FX-112 is a 60-watt digital modelling guitar combo amplifier, introduced around 2000, combining Yamaha's renowned DG-Stomp digital preamp technology with a solid-state power amplifier and a single 12-inch Eminence/Celestion speaker.

It offers a wide range of vintage and modern amplifier simulations, with 90 factory presets and 90 user-programmable patches.
Its built-in digital effects include compressor, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, rotary speaker, digital delay, tape echo and three types of reverb.

Key features:
- 60 W solid-state power amplifier.
- 12-inch Eminence/Celestion speaker.
- 8 digital amplifier models.
- 16 speaker cabinet simulations (for line-out connections).
- 180 programmable sounds (90 factory + 90 user).
- MIDI In/Out and digital output.
- Built-in chromatic tuner and speaker simulation.
- Dedicated four-button foot controller included.

In short: A versatile, digitally modelled guitar amplifier offering extensive tonal flexibility and effects without requiring a separate pedalboard.
It can also function as a preamp for external amplification or direct recording.

Since there is no USB connection or application available for these devices, it is a bit hard to keep track of your preferred settings and sounds.
The only way would be to backup your presets using SysEx dumps (which are not documented).
The goal of this mobile app is to have a visible source of your presets that you can then manually dial in at your amp.

Presets can be stored to the localStorage of the browser (Android/Chrome) and exported, imported and shared using a basic JSON file.
It comes with bunch of (more or less) useful demo presets.

The PWA is accessible via this URL: https://mickeylux.github.io/dg60fx/
